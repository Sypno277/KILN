Built in

Built in: works right after install. The built-in engines include ImageMagick, PDFium, PDFsharp, rhwp, Kiln HWPX writer, 7-Zip…

Optional engine

Optional engine: a free download inside Kiln (Settings → Engines), saved in Kiln's own folder and removable at any time. If the engine is already on your PC (on the PATH or in Program Files), Kiln uses that instead; Settings → Engines → Use my copy picks a specific one.
